This is the Project files for the Short Drivers submission to Jame Gam

Unzip and use Unity Hub to access the project

CONTROLS

Steering- Left click and drag the steering wheel to rotate it

There is 2 black pedals below the steering wheel, the one on the left is the brake and the one on the right is the gas, you need to click on them to brake or go forward

drag the shifter until it clicks into place to change gears or go backwards.
